    Simulate Production Plan
    Tells the system how many cabinets of each type your shop builds per year. This is the foundation of the Parts Analytics — without a production plan, the system can't calculate how many parts you'll need or when you'll run out.

    Example: If you build 120 base cabinets per year, the system automatically knows you'll need 480 side panels in the next 4 months and tells you if your current stock is enough.
